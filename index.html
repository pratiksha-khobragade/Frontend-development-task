Features

Everything your team needs to reply faster

Simple tools that remove busywork, so agents can focus on the customer.

Unified inbox

Email, chat and social DMs land in one place. No more switching tabs.

AI reply suggestions

Get a ready-to-edit draft based on your past answers and help articles.

Team collaboration

Assign tickets, leave private notes and see who's replying in real time.

Live analytics

Track response time, satisfaction and workload with clear dashboards.

SLA reminders

Automatic alerts before a ticket breaches your response-time promise.

Secure by default

Encrypted data, role-based access and full audit logs for peace of mind.

Up and running in three simple steps

Pulsedesk is a web app, so there is nothing to install. Most teams are live within an afternoon.

  1. 1

    Connect your channels

    Link your support email, website chat and social accounts in a few clicks.

  2. 2

    Invite your team

    Add agents, set roles and create simple rules to route tickets automatically.

  3. 3

    Reply and improve

    Answer from one inbox, use AI suggestions, and learn from your weekly reports.
